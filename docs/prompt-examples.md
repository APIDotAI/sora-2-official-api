# Prompt examples for Sora 2 Official

These prompts are written for testing the Sora 2 Official API through APIDot. They are starting points for product teams, not guaranteed outputs. Adjust brand names, subject details, source URLs, and safety rules before production use.

## 1. Cinematic prompt video

A cinematic tracking shot of a compact electric car driving through neon-lit rain, realistic reflections, slow camera push, dramatic product commercial style.

## 2. Image-guided product teaser

Animate the product from the reference image as a premium launch teaser, slow dolly-in, clean studio lighting, realistic reflections, polished campaign video.

## 3. Short social clip

A cozy kitchen scene where a smart coffee maker starts brewing at sunrise, gentle camera movement, realistic steam, warm commercial tone.

## 4. Storyboard scene

A detective enters a quiet train station at night, noir lighting, slow reveal, cinematic framing, subtle camera movement, tense atmosphere.

## 5. Brand film moment

A founder places a prototype on a studio table, lights turn on around it, calm cinematic pacing, premium documentary style.

## 6. Reference composition

Use the supplied image as composition reference, animate a clean product reveal with controlled motion and realistic reflections.

## Production prompt notes

- Store prompt versions together with task_id, model, user/job owner, and request payload.
- Keep source media URLs reachable long enough for APIDot to fetch them.
- Be explicit about camera motion, subject continuity, scene pacing, audio intent, duration, resolution, and aspect ratio.
- Do not log prompts or source media URLs if they may contain private customer data.
